Small-Pot Rice Noodle

Small-pot rice noodle easily identifiable from the deeply coloured spicy 8h broth takes its name from being cooked in the traditional Yunnan copper pot. The specially marinated pork mince and fresh tofu provide the base of the flavour with Yun pickles and chilli oil finishing off the flavour profiles.
